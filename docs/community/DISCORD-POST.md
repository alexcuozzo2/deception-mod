## Title

Deception

## Body — copy below

**What if the army at your border was a bluff?**

Deception adds a new tech branch to **The Battle of Polytopia**, built around disguises, espionage and information warfare.

**Mimics & disguises**
Recruit cheap, 1-HP Mimics that copy other units’ appearances and movement—but cannot deal damage. Regroup to adopt a disguise and optionally swap positions, pass appearances to allied units, or smuggle resources home. That dragon might be a dragon. Or it might cost one star.

**Spies & Blackmail**
Subvert enemy units and temporarily command them as Rogues. Blackmail a visible enemy unit to siphon income from its home city, then increase the pressure on later turns. Is that perfectly positioned bomber worth keeping if it’s funding your opponent?

**Reconnaissance & Surveillance**
See which technologies your opponents know and toggle a view of their fog. Find out what they can see—and what they can’t.

**Foggers & Trade Secrets**
Flying Foggers throw smoke grenades to cover explored tiles with fog again, with up to two Blackout shots per turn. Trade Secrets turns technologies your opponents have and you lack into income.

The branch also includes **Mermimics and Spymaids for Aquarion**, custom artwork, sound effects and disguise inspection controls.

**Status:** experimental and being prepared for PolyMod review. I’d love feedback on the concept, balance and implementation. Current platform support and multiplayer limitations are documented on GitHub.

**Source & details:** https://github.com/alexcuozzo2/deception-mod
