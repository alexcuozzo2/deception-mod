   appearance variant. This display reduction must not remove authoritative
   variant unlocks or make recruitment fail again.

   wheel rather than just close the original. Preserve the selected city's
   position through the transition, and preserve own-roster gating and Back.

v1.6 scope/roster constraints remain unchanged. Cancel/Back spends nothing;
choosing a form enters the ordinary confirmation; no automatic purchase occurs.
