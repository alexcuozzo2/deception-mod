## User-reported successes
The Deception popup remained compact, the second radial wheel appeared, and the
Warrior-form purchase completed. Alex also reports recruiting all the available
unit forms he tried except Cloaks. This is not evidence that every tribe/form was
exhaustively tested. Research name/chime and unlock gating were verified in earlier
user tests and must not regress.

## User-reported gaps
- Mimics cannot be disbanded.
- Mimics cannot claim ruins or villages.
- Mimics cannot embark in ports.
- They show a red 1 HP even though their real 1/1 HP is full.
- The owner's ordinary view exposes the real 1 HP instead of the normal-looking
  apparent unit and apparent full health.
- The second wheel's Back control uses a question-mark icon.
- The first wheel's generic Mimic category looks like an ordinary Warrior;
  use a distinctive icon, or a clearly marked placeholder, not disguised Warrior art.
- Cloak imitation is unavailable; the current source explicitly defers it.

## UI-A04: latest explicit user instruction, not silently folded into v1.5
User wording:
> Also, they appeared to the user as a 1hp unit, when in reality they should appear
> to me the same way that they appear to the opponent (as the normal, full health
> unit). One when tapping twice on the unit should it show the 1hp mimic sprite
> as the underlying unit, and on the corresponding underlying unit information popup.

The typo "One" is retained; the intended contrast is ordinary apparent display
versus deliberately inspecting the underlying Mimic on the second selection.

This changes older spec §2.4 and DEC-010/011, which said first selection Actual,
second selection Appearance, with the map always apparent. Those old paragraphs
and tests remain preserved verbatim in `project/spec`; they are not the latest
instruction for Mimic presentation. Update both implementation and acceptance
expectations explicitly rather than passing stale tests.

Required behavior for the next Mimic presentation increment:
1. Default/unselected and first ordinary selection use apparent form and apparent
   HP, including matching native healthy/injured coloring. A freshly recruited
   Warrior-form Mimic appears as a full-health Warrior, while real HP stays 1/1.
2. A second intentional selection of the same controlled Mimic exposes the actual
   Mimic sprite/portrait and the corresponding real 1/1 HP information privately.
3. This is a local inspection change, not an authoritative disguise reveal. Enemy
   views, replay-visible events and other clients must not see a true-form flash.
4. Inspection spends no actions/stars, changes no real or apparent HP, and does not
   re-spawn the unit or grant offensive abilities. Preserve authorization and
   targeting guards. Keep the original distinction from an OS-timed double-click:
   use repeat-selection semantics unless Alex explicitly requests a timed gesture.
5. Reset the Mimic inspection to apparent on a new selection context. Define and
   test the subsequent-toggle behavior consistently. This reset is the natural
   completion of the new default, not a separate user-provided corner-case decision.
6. The second-tap sprite must be confined to the local view. The exact widget
   (local selected-unit rendering versus inspection portrait) should be implemented
   explicitly and reported; do not silently turn it into a public world-state edit.

Do NOT interpret "full health" as forcing every apparent record permanently to
max HP. New recruitment initializes apparent health at its native maximum;
subsequent apparent injury, feigned recovery and copied state still follow the spec.
Do NOT equate apparent information display with automatically granting feigned
commands, hiding basic movement, or giving the Mimic the apparent unit's weapons.
The old PanelMode enum couples command menus to inspection; that coupling needs review.

## Other requirements remain unchanged
Non-Rogue eligible Mimics may collect ruins, claim neutral villages and capture
eligible enemy cities subject to native timing/operative-form rules (v1.5 §8).
Rogues retain their capture/ruins/disband bans. Ordinary disband uses genuine paid
investment, never the apparent expensive unit's price (v1.5 §7.3; DEC-029).
Normal embark/disembark must preserve actual Mimic identity/HP, action history,
passenger state and independent apparent vessel (v1.5 §7); do not merely delete
the water guard and let a native transition manufacture a genuine warship.
All tribes and solo-head Centipede restrictions remain governed by v1.6 A01.
No Polibrary dependency is requested for this custom mod.

## What is source-confirmed versus still undiagnosed
Cloak/Mermaid Cloak/Mooni/Moth/Aquapult are explicit deferrals in RecruitmentCatalog.
Naval/ice-water entry is deliberately blocked by NoPrototypeBoarding, and the
prototype removes embarksTo. These are temporary implementation boundaries, not
approved final design restrictions.
No native disband or capture adapter is installed in RecruitmentHooks.Install;
Core.Rules.CanCapture is only model logic. The exact native rejection predicates
for the reported disband/capture failures have NOT been traced. Diagnose before
adding permissive overrides. The precise red-health renderer condition also has
NOT been traced. The report has no screenshots or click-level trace of these failures.
